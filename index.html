Maike Osborne

AI expert, AI entrepreneur, and AI speaker

  • Professor of Machine Learning at the University of Oxford, UK
  • Co-founder of Mind Foundry, which has raised $48 million to build AI for high-stakes applications
  • World-leading academic on how AI is transforming the future of work

“one of the most influential studies of our time”

– the BBC's Rory Cellan-Jones, on Carl Frey and Maike’s paper from 2013

Maike Osborne is, firstly, an engineer, who has helped build AI to contribute to quantum computing, astrostatistics and battery modelling. She is also at the forefront of work that analyses the economic and social impacts of AI—she has advised ministers in Germany and Japan on AI’s impacts, with her methodology also being used by Obama’s council of economic advisors and Teresa May’s Taylor Report.
