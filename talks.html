Talks

Maike regularly gives keynotes, fireside chats, and interactive sessions on AI and the future of work for select audiences all over the world, on topics including the following.

  • The Future of Work, discussing how AI is transforming industries, job markets, and the skills required for the future.
  • Ethics and Governance of AI, exploring the ethical considerations, societal impacts, and regulatory challenges of rapidly-changing AI technologies.
  • Responsible High-Stakes Applications of AI, through the lens of Mind Foundry’s real-world deployments, the challenges of deploying AI responsibly for applications in which you cannot afford to be wrong—such as in insurance, infrastructure and national security.

For more information on Maike’s speaking engagements, please contact Karen O'Donnell at Chartwell Speakers.
